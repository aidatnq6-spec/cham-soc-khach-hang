BAN NEXT.JS + TYPESCRIPT + FIREBASE

1. Cai Node.js 24 LTS (khuyen nghi).
2. Mo PowerShell tai thu muc du an, chay npm.cmd ci.
3. Kiem tra .env.local, xem .env.example va README.md.
4. Bam MO_PHAN_MEM.bat hoac chay npm.cmd run dev.
5. Mo http://localhost:4173 sau khi terminal hien Ready.

Xem tren dien thoai cung Wi-Fi: bam MO_TREN_DIEN_THOAI.bat.
Clipboard/PWA can HTTPS hoac localhost; HTTP LAN co the can copy thu cong.
Khong mo file index.html va khong dung Python http.server cho ban moi.
Ma nguon ban cu da duoc go; co the tra cuu trong lich su Git.
Khong deploy Firebase Hosting tinh tu thu muc goc. Xem README.md de trien khai production.
